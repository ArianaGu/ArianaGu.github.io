Ongoing Research
======
* **Pre-trained Diffusion Models for Optimization-Guided Deconvolution with Large Kernels** &mdash; with Alex Mehta, Ajil Jalal, Amit Kohli, Laura Waller.
  * Applied diffusion posterior sampling methods (DPS, Red-Diff, DDNM, EDM) to deconvolution problems with diffuser PSFs, identifying significant hallucination issues in existing methods.
  * Investigating an optimization-inspired purification method to alleviate the trade-off between visual plausibility and measurement data fidelity.

* **Automatic Regularization for Label-Free Computational Microscopy** &mdash; Imaging Residency with [Biohub](https://biohub.org/), in collaboration with the waveOrder team at Biohub SF &nbsp;·&nbsp; May 2026 &ndash; present.
  * Learning regularization parameters directly from raw measurements, removing hand-tuning from the reconstruction pipeline.
  * Scaling [waveOrder](https://github.com/mehta-lab/waveorder) to larger and more diverse datasets.
